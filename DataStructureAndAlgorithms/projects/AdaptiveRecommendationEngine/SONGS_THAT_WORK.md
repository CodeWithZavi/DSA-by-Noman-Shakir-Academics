## Why Some Songs Might NOT Work

### ❌ Very New Releases (< 1 week old)
- ACRCloud database updates weekly
- Brand new songs might not be indexed yet
- **Wait**: 1-2 weeks after release

### ❌ Underground/Independent Artists
- Small independent labels
- SoundCloud-only releases
- YouTube-only uploads
- **Example**: Local rappers, bedroom producers

### ❌ Remixes/Bootlegs
- Unofficial remixes
- DJ mixes
- Mashups
- **Example**: Random YouTube remixes

### ❌ Live Performances
- Concert recordings
- Live sessions
- Acoustic versions (if not officially released)

### ❌ Regional/Local Music
- Very local artists with limited distribution
- Songs only on regional platforms
- **Example**: Local Punjabi artists not on major platforms

## What Affects Recognition

### ✅ Better Recognition
- **High volume** (70-100%)
- **Clear audio** (no background noise)
- **Close microphone** (< 1 meter from speaker)
- **Good speaker quality**
- **Popular songs** (millions of streams)

### ❌ Worse Recognition
- **Low volume** (< 50%)
- **Background noise** (talking, traffic)
- **Far microphone** (> 2 meters)
- **Phone speaker** (poor quality)
- **Obscure songs** (< 100k streams)
